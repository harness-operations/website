## The operational layer around harnesses

Harness engineering focuses on making individual agents effective. Harness Operations addresses the systems problem that appears when harnesses and their executions must be observed, coordinated, governed, secured, constrained, intervened in, and audited together.

The model is deliberately **harness-centric**. Different harnesses can expose different session lifecycles, permission mechanisms, capabilities, persistence models, and execution environments. Interoperability should preserve those differences where they carry operational meaning rather than force every harness into one generic lifecycle.

## Relationship to AgentOps

`AgentOps` and `agent operations` are emerging umbrella terms for production practices around AI agents, but their scope and terminology are not yet standardized. Harness Operations is **adjacent to and overlapping with** that practice while addressing a more specific systems problem: operating heterogeneous harnesses and their executions coherently.

## Deliberately small

Reference Model 0.1 does not define a new wire protocol, a common Harness lifecycle, a centralized control-plane requirement, a registry, or a certification program. Existing standards and established operational disciplines should be composed where they already solve the problem.
